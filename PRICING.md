## FAQ

**What counts as a "seat"?**
Each developer who uses ctx-pilot on their machine counts as one seat.

**Can I try before I buy?**
Yes. ctx-pilot works fully without a license. The commercial license is an honor system for companies above the revenue threshold.

**What if my company grows past $1M?**
Great problem to have! Reach out and we'll set up a commercial license.

**Do you offer discounts for larger teams?**
Yes. Contact us for volume pricing on 20+ seats.

We want ctx-pilot to be free for indie developers, students, and small teams. The commercial license ensures larger companies who benefit from the tool contribute to its development.
